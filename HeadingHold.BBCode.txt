[h1]✈️ Heading Hold[/h1]
Tired of watching your cargo and passenger flights pull off radical 90-degree banking maneuvers the exact second their wheels leave the tarmac? Sick of planes deciding your downtown skyscrapers make great slalom poles right after takeoff? 

[b]Heading Hold[/b] is here to bring aviation regulations to Cities: Skylines.

[h2]What it does:[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Strict Runway Heading:[/b] Forces commercial cargo and passenger planes to strictly maintain runway heading and wings-level attitude during initial climb.
[*] [b]No Tail-Strikes:[/b] Prevents sudden, jerky yaw changes while keeping natural pitch intact (most of the time).
[*] [b]Smart Filtering:[/b] Only applies to [b]CargoPlaneAI[/b] and [b]PassengerPlaneAI[/b]—private aircraft are left alone to fly however they want.
[*] [b]Clean Handoff at 100m AGL:[/b] Once the aircraft safely clears the airport environment (100 meters above ground level), the mod steps aside, and hands navigation back to the filed flight plan!
[/list]

[h2]Technical Details[/h2]
[list]
[*] Built using Harmony.
[*] Zero performance overhead during cruising or city management.
[*] Compatible with vanilla, DLC, and custom workshop passenger/cargo planes.
[/list]
